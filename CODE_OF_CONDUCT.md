# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Contributors and maintainers commit to a harassment-free, welcoming environment regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give actionable technical feedback, accept correction, and keep discussions focused on the project and its users. Harassment, threats, discrimination, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
